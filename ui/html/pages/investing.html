Automated Investing

A bot buys the dip whenever one of a selected group of ETFs or crypto coins drops in price over the past 24 hours.

How it works

  1. Reserves. Each ETF and coin holds its own cash reserve, topped up by a fixed amount once a day.
  2. Price change. If the price went up, nothing happens and the reserve keeps growing. If it went down, a proportion of the reserve is spent on a purchase.
  3. Proportion of the reserve. The further the price drops, the bigger that proportion.
  • 🐂

    During a bull run the reserves keep growing, so even a small dip triggers a large purchase. This avoids cash drag in a hot market.

  • 🐻

    During a bear market the reserves are drawn down steadily, a slice at a time. This avoids catching a falling knife in one go.

Every 0.25% of decline commits another 5% of the reserve. A drop past 4.74% — rare for a broad sector fund — spends the reserve entirely.

Example

A fund's reserve is sitting at $12.40 and it closes the day down 1.10%. That lands on the 1.25% rung, so the bot commits 25% of the reserve: a $3.10 buy, leaving $9.30 in the reserve.
